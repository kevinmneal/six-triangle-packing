# Project guidance

Start with README.md, the preprint, and docs/VERIFICATION.md. Keep verification separate from numerical discovery.

Preserve the accepted certificate and reviewed consumer bytes. Source hashes identify versions; do not silently update a manifest to accept an unreviewed mathematical change. Use new versions for substantive proof changes.

Retain arbitrary independent rotations, all closed boundary cases, the original-frame target-symmetry argument, and explicit unresolved status. Do not infer optimality from a numerical search, browser demo, or local rigidity alone. Do not claim Lean formalization or human peer review.

Use collaborators selectively. Preserve unrelated changes. Do not commit, push, or publish without user authorization. Keep evolving progress in HANDOFF.md.
