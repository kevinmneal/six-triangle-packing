# Attribution and AI-use disclosure

The attaining six-triangle construction is credited to **Maurizio Morandi, August 2008**, through [Erich Friedman's catalogue](https://erich-friedman.github.io/packing/triintri/). This project claims an unrestricted optimality proof and its exact computational certificate, not discovery of that construction. The project also relies on the published literature identified in the preprint and literature audit for historical context.

Kevin M. Neal directed the research and is the named human author of the preprint. **GPT-6 Astra in OpenAI Codex**, together with **ChatGPT Pro**, was used extensively for mathematical exploration, argument generation and refinement, code generation, manuscript drafting, and adversarial implementation reviews. The separately implemented exact consumers were produced within that workflow. The author takes responsibility for the released claims. No external human peer-review acceptance is claimed.

“Independent implementation” means that a consumer separately implements the specified arithmetic and geometry without calling the corresponding production routines. It does not mean a blind review, an unrelated proof strategy, independence from a model family, or a proof-kernel formalization. The second global review explicitly reuses the earlier independent local verifier; that reuse is documented in the source manifests.

[Yohei Nakajima's soft-to-rigid packing presentation](https://yoheinakajima.github.io/soft-to-rigid-packing/) inspired the idea of pairing a preprint and exact data with an accessible interactive explanation. This project does not claim to have applied that method to prove triangle optimality. No source code or artwork from that website is incorporated.

The [11SquaresFormalized project](https://github.com/Queuingtheorydotcom/11SquaresFormalized) is a useful adjacent precedent for explicit verification scope and source provenance. We inspected its reported result and trust model, but did not replay its full Lean development and do not claim an equivalent formalization here.
